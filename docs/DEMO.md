# Demo (3 minutes)

Setup: laptop on the projector with the web app and the agent timeline visible. Daniyal plays the front desk on his
own phone, on speaker next to the mic. Backup: screen recording of steps 2 to 6.

1. 0:00 Hook: "Booking still means phone calls, and agents that do it for you either freeze or guess. Tandem does
   the work and asks you when it matters."
2. 0:15 Clinic, "Dentist near me for a cleaning next week". Shortlist with Phone / Online badges. Pick the phone clinic.
3. 0:30 Consent card. Leave date of birth OFF (it is not even on the card). Approve.
4. 0:40 Live call. Daniyal follows the front desk script below. Two cards appear: the new time (Accept) and the
   Guardian block on date of birth (Don't share).
5. 1:45 Verifier: Confirmed. Calendar file.
6. 1:55 Online clinic: slot card, review card, booked through Cal.com, visible in the Cal.com dashboard.
7. 2:35 Switch to Hotel: show its consent fields and decision types.
8. 2:50 Close: "Six agents, two channels, three niches, and the human decides every commitment."

If time is short, drop step 7, then step 6.

## Front desk script for Daniyal (clinic)
- Answer: "Bright Smile Dental, how can I help?"
- When asked for a morning slot: "Mornings are full next week. I can do Tuesday at 3 PM."
- After the agent comes back: "Great. Can I also get his date of birth for the file?"
- When the agent reads back the time: "Yes, that's booked."

## No answer variant (optional, shows error handling)
Do not tap the time card. The agent asks: "Could you hold Tuesday 3 PM for 15 minutes?" Daniyal: "Sure."
Outcome Tentative with the hold; tap the open offer to trigger the call back.
